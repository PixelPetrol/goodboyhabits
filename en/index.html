Every habit is a paw.

A glass of water in the morning, ten pages at night. One tap ticks it off, the day ring fills up, and when it is all done Rudeusz jumps for joy.

Your times of day

Morning, afternoon, evening. Every day, on chosen days or a few times a week.

Reminders

At a set time, or after something you do anyway: after coffee, after work.

Tasks alongside

With a due date and a checklist. A morning reminder of what is due today.

Bad day?
That's fine.

Your streak has one spare day a week, and you can make up for yesterday today. You never lose your paws. Rudeusz never gets cross and never talks. He just waits for you.

A history worth looking at.

Every day is a dot. You see streaks, weaker weeks and what goes best. A short recap at the end of each month.

A budget, if you want one.

Spending, limits and regular payments in their own tab, with receipt photos. Złoty, euro, dollar or your own currency. Don't need it? One switch turns it off.

Paws unlock new places.

36 trophies and new spots for Rudeusz: the kitchen, the sofa, a cloud. Seasonal ones arrive in winter.
