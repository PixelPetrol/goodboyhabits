Rudeusz is waiting for you to tick a habit.

A habit app with a 3D dog. He sits by his bowl until you do something, and jumps when today's list is done.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

One paw for every habit

In the morning you tick a glass of water, at night ten pages of a book. Each tick is a paw and a slice of the day ring. At half Rudeusz is happy, when everything is done he jumps, and under the ring you see how many paws he still needs.

When you want

Every day, on chosen weekdays, on set days of the month, or 3 times a week on any days.

After what

You note what the habit follows, for example "after coffee". The reminder comes at the time you pick.

Today's tasks

Tasks have a due date and a checklist. At 8:00 your phone tells you what is due today.

One missed day does not reset your streak

The first missed day each week is forgiven, and you can still tick yesterday's habit today. Nobody takes back the paws you earned. After two empty days Rudeusz falls asleep. When you come back he wakes up, and you get an extra paw as a welcome. If you prefer strict rules, turn on strict mode.

Every habit, day by day

History shows a dot for each day: done, skipped or missed. In the first week of a month you get a recap of the last one, with your active days and the habit that went best.

Spending in the same app

Add an expense with a category and a photo of the receipt, set monthly limits and regular payments. The day before rent is due you get a reminder. Don't need a budget? Switch the Spending tab off in settings.

Paws unlock new places

Rudeusz starts in a meadow. At 10 paws you unlock a room, then the sofa, the kitchen, and at 220 paws a cloud. Seasonal backgrounds arrive in autumn and December. There are 36 trophies, from your first paw to a year of activity.

Backgrounds from the app

Tick habits from your home screen

The medium and large widgets list today's habits: tap the circle and the paw counts. After 6 pm Rudeusz waits by his bowl on the widget, and from 10 pm he sleeps. On iPhone there is also a Lock Screen widget. Ticking from a widget works on Android and on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Everything stays on your phone

No sign-up

Open the app and add your first habit straight away. You never give an e-mail address.

Backup to a file

You make a backup yourself, whenever you want, and keep it wherever you like: a drive, your e-mail or a cloud folder.

No ads or analytics

The app sends us nothing about what you do in it.

For now your data lives on one phone. Syncing between phones is planned for later.
