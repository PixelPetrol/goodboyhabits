What it does

🐕

A dog who is alive

Rudeusz breathes, wags his tail and is happy for every paw, all in 3D. You can pet him with a finger. He never talks and never shames you.

🗓️

Habits your way

Every day, on chosen weekdays, on days of the month or a few times a week. A break for holidays in one tap.

🔥

A gentle streak

One spare day a week, and you can make up for yesterday. You never lose your paws.

✅

Tasks

With a due date and a checklist. A morning reminder of what is due today.

💰

A budget, if you want one

Spending, income, category limits, regular payments and a photo of the receipt. Złoty, euro, dollar or your own currency.

🏆

Trophies and backgrounds

36 trophies and backgrounds unlocked with paws, plus seasonal ones in winter.

📱

Widgets

Small, medium and large on the home screen, and on the iPhone Lock Screen. Tick habits without opening the app.

🔒

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone, with a backup file whenever you want.

🌍

Four languages

English, Polish, German and Spanish. Light and dark theme.

Take a look

Good Boy Plus

The app is complete for free. Plus adds extras: colour themes, a whole year in History, several photos per entry and soft tick sounds. No random rewards and no hidden subscriptions.

Your data is yours

There is no account and no server. Habits, tasks, entries and photos are stored only on your phone. The app collects no statistics and shows no ads. Privacy policy
