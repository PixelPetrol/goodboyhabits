Privacy policy

Good Boy Habits · Last updated: 17 November 2026.

In short

Good Boy Habits does not collect any of your data. There is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking tools. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Who is responsible for the app

The app is made and published by PixelPetrol. A contact address will be added before the app is published.

What data the app holds

Habits, tasks, spending and income entries, accounts, budgets, settings and any receipt photos you attach. This data is stored only in your phone's storage.

We never send it anywhere and we have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data from your phone.

Backups

You create a backup file yourself, whenever you want. The file goes wherever you save or send it (for example a drive or an e-mail). Your phone may also include the app's data in its own backup (iCloud, Google), depending on your system settings.

Permissions

Notifications: reminders about habits, tasks and payments. Your phone creates them itself; nothing is sent from outside. You can turn them off in the app and in your phone's settings.

Camera and photos: only when you add a receipt photo to an entry yourself. The photo stays in the app on your phone.

Widgets

The widget shows today's habits. The app stores the data it needs in storage shared by the app and the widget on your phone. It never leaves the device.

Purchases

If you buy Good Boy Plus, the payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play under their terms. We do not receive your payment details.

Rating the app

The app may ask once for a rating using the App Store or Google Play system dialog. The rating goes straight to the store.

Your rights

Because we do not process your personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to share, correct or delete. You keep full control on your phone: you can export your data, restore it from a backup or delete it (You → Backup and data → Start from scratch).

Changes

If sign-in or syncing between phones is added in the future, we will update this policy before the change and describe what data is sent and why.
